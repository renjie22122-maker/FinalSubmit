\chapter{Implementation and Reproducibility Record}\label{app:evidence-record}

\section{Evaluated components}

The empirical study uses four principal implementations: the London
data-preparation workflow, the Sat3DGen large-image extension, the active mesh
assembly package, and the ChordAtlas integration layer.
The project source code and submission package are available at
\url{https://github.com/renjie22122-maker/FinalSubmit}.
Runtime import and entry-point tracing identify the implementation used for
each experiment.  The published Sat3DGen,
ChordAtlas, BigSUR, FrankenGAN, CityEngine, and Ubiq systems are referenced in
the chapters where their methods or interfaces are discussed.

\section{Model-example comparison record}

The representation comparison is generated from the retained
\texttt{modelsExample} directory.  Only the \texttt{BigImageModel} and
\texttt{satelliteImageModel} request/result packages provide directly matched
identity: both name OSM way~369245408 and contain the same target bounds and
footprint geometry.  Their building OBJ files provide the geometry, topology,
and vertex-colour evidence, while the result manifests provide the recorded DSM
status and coverage reported in
Section~\ref{sec:matched-building-comparison}.

The CityEngine directory retains a CityEngine 2026 export log, OBJ, MTL, and
three texture images, but no common OSM identifier.  The two route-labelled
ChordAtlas directories retain OBJ/MTL outputs and diffuse/normal/specular map
sets; the OBJ files do not embed the footprint identity.  The standalone
ChordAtlas directory has no matching location or run manifest and is used only
as an illustrative reference.  The reproducible audit and software renderer
are stored in \texttt{research/scripts}; their JSON/CSV outputs are stored in
\texttt{research/results/models\_example}.  The rendered comparison figures use
derived vertex colour or stored material maps and do not embed the source
satellite mosaic.

\section{Matched satellite-derived outputs}

The \texttt{modelsExample2} record provides a second geographically matched
case for the large-image and independent-tile routes.  The two request
packages contain identical selection bounds and footprint geometry for OSM
ways~33719501 and~1455280501.  The large-image route processes a contiguous
4-by-4 satellite mosaic through 100 overlapping inference windows, whereas
the independent route assembles 20 separately generated meshes.  Both result
packages record successful DSM sampling for all vertices in the extracted
building assets.

\section{Six representations of the selected building}

The \texttt{modelsExample2} record contains six representations of the same
selected London Trocadero building.  They comprise the large-image and
independent-tile Sat3DGen models, the corresponding ChordAtlas outputs, the
CityEngine model, and the original ChordAtlas model.  The two Sat3DGen result
manifests identify the building as OSM way~33719501.  The later OBJ/MTL exports
belong to the same selected case, although they do not repeat the OSM
identifier inside the model files.

The two Sat3DGen routes retain dense, irregular satellite-conditioned surfaces
and encode appearance as per-vertex colour.  Their corresponding ChordAtlas
outputs replace vertex colour with UV-mapped diffuse, normal, and specular
material structures while maintaining a similar horizontal building extent.
The CityEngine model provides the most compact and regular envelope; its doors
and windows are represented in shared image materials rather than as separate
openings.  The ChordAtlas packages contain independently materialised facade
regions, including window-labelled materials and, in the independent-tile
output, door-labelled materials.  These differences describe geometry and
appearance representation; greater face or material counts do not alone imply
greater reconstruction accuracy.

Table~\ref{tab:models-example-2-six-packages} describes the stored geometry
and appearance representation in every package.  Face counts are reported as
triangles after OBJ triangulation, except that the CityEngine row also retains
its original polygon-record count.
